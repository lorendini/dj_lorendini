ABOUT

Who is behind
the console

I’m Lorenzo, aka Lorendini. I’m a DJ and music selector, and music has always been part of my life: from clubs to weddings, from private parties to events.

I’m passionate about vinyl, hi-fi and music culture. Above all, I care about one thing: choosing the right music for the right moment.

No forced entertainment formulas: I focus on music selection, sound and reading the dancefloor.
